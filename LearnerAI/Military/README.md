# Military Module

Military translates strategic and informational requirements into useful army behavior.

## Owns

- minimum defensive army;
- counter composition;
- production demand;
- army readiness;
- attack reserve;
- attack lifecycle;
- retreat;
- reinforcement;
- siege;
- military recovery.

## Product role

The player must distinguish standing defense from expendable attack force.

A fight must not consume the force required to survive the next five minutes.

Feudal military should remain a minimum credible commitment unless Strategy deliberately chooses continued pressure.

## First competitive target

Support the Castle trajectory while answering observed pressure.

Then, in Castle, scale production from actual army requirements rather than simply accumulating military buildings.

Military may translate Information and Strategy into military demands.

It does not silently rewrite strategic posture.
