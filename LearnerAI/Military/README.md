# Military Module

Military translates strategic and informational requirements into useful army behavior.

## Owns

- minimum defensive army;
- counter composition;
- army readiness;
- attack reserve;
- attack lifecycle;
- retreat;
- reinforcement;
- siege;
- naval military posture;
- transport/landing protection;
- military recovery.

## Product role

The player must distinguish standing defense from expendable attack force.

A fight must not consume the force required to survive the next five minutes.

Feudal military should remain a minimum credible commitment unless Strategy deliberately chooses continued pressure.

Siege is not decoration. It exists to solve specific positional problems such as buildings, massed ranged units, fortified positions, and late-game conversion.

## Monks and relics

Military may consume strategic Monk/relic demands created by Strategy.

It must distinguish:

- monk production;
- escort/readiness;
- relic collection;
- relic denial;
- healing/conversion support;
- recovery after monk loss.

## Water

Military must support naval defense/offense and transport protection when the map makes water strategically relevant.

A water plan can be abandoned deliberately when its opportunity cost exceeds its value.
