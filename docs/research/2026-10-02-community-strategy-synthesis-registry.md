## Corpus classification

Every future community pattern should move through:

`DISCOVERED -> CORROBORATED -> SEMANTICIZED -> EXPRESSIBLE -> LOWERABLE -> VERIFIED`

Corroboration must account for historical lineage. Ten copies of one ancestor are not ten independent sources.

The current implementation intentionally does not encode unproven native placement heuristics, transport completion, target liveness, or naval-control proof as facts.

Those remain explicit runtime research items.
