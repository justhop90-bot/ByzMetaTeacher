# Production and Military Composition Behavioral Contract

## Purpose

Encode the community practice that production capacity follows standing strategic demand, while military composition follows battlefield roles rather than static unit counts.

## Production contract

`standing demand -> required role -> provider capacity -> queue admission -> unit witness -> replacement`

A production building is a capability provider.

A queue entry is pending work.

A completed unit is a world-state witness.

Exact birth/queue-exit timing remains runtime evidence.

## Composition roles

The stock Byzantine strategy tracks:

- defensive floor;
- anti-mounted;
- anti-ranged;
- anti-infantry;
- mobile pressure;
- siege support;
- naval defense;
- attack reserve;
- replacement capacity.

The attack controller may consume only units above the protected standing floor.

## Community-derived composition policy

- Orthogonal threat classes may coexist.
- Same-class counter packages arbitrate deterministically.
- Composition changes when threat evidence changes.
- Upgrades and role coverage matter more than raw unit-count parity.
- Siege becomes active when a real ranged/fortification/positional problem exists.
- Premium Byzantine units are a strategic conversion, not an unconditional unit preference.

## Current stock synthesis

The broader profile now includes:

- Castle Knight floor;
- Castle Cataphract floor;
- Castle siege-response floor;
- bounded Monk floor;
- Castle Mangonel support when sustained pressure justifies it;
- Imperial Bombard Cannon conversion against enemy fortification.

Existing counter packages remain the authoritative threat-specific response layer.

## Capacity scaling

Additional providers should be opened only when current plus pending demand can justify additional throughput.

Do not build production buildings for their own sake.

## Recovery

Lost military changes replacement demand.

Lost providers reopen capability demand.

A tactical defeat does not automatically invalidate strategic intent.

## Acceptance

Every production/composition package requires:

- role owner;
- threat evidence;
- provider;
- capacity target;
- queue/pending semantics;
- unit witness;
- release/replacement;
- native fixture;
- deterministic compilation.
